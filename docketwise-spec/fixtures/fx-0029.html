Custom Intakes

Build your own intakes with custom questions

June 16, 2026
Table of contents

Please Note: Because we are constantly improving our platform and updating our branding, the interface, labels, and visuals you see in your account may differ slightly from the screenshots or descriptions in this article.

Overview

In Docketwise, you can use our Custom Intakes option to:

  • Build unlimited questionnaire templates for different use cases.

  • Ask anything you want by including any combination of questions:

    • DocketWise's pre-made questions, for which responses will be automatically saved to a contact and/or

    • Craft your own Custom Questions.

  • Organize Custom Questions into Custom Tabs.

  • Include document upload requests with upload buttons for your client (learn more here).

  • Share easily via Email, SMS Text, Shareable link, and Portal invitation.

  • Translate the Intakes into Spanish, Portuguese, French, Hindi, Russian, Mandarin Chinese, Arabic, Korean, Turkish, and Haitian Creole. (Only available for pre-made questions and not Custom Questions).

Creating a New Custom Intake Template

To get started building your Custom Intake, you will need to follow the steps below:

  1. Navigate to Settings > Custom Intakes.

  2. Click the New Intake + button.

  3. Name your Custom Intake template.

  4. Click the Create Intake Template → button.

Editing a Custom Intake Template

In order to make edits to an already-existing Custom Intake Template, you will need to follow the steps below:

  1. Navigate to Settings > Custom Intakes.

  2. Click on the pencil icon next to the Custom Intake Template that you would like to edit.

Adding Questions to a Custom Intake Template

Once you have made a Custom Intake Template, you are able to ask any questions you want by utilizing our pre-made questions and custom questions.

Adding DocketWise's Pre-Made Questions

When building a Custom Intake Template, you can choose from any of Docketwise's pre-made questions, for which the responses will be automatically saved to the contact and thereafter synced to the contact's other forms in Docketwise; these questions can be found under General, Immigration, Family, and Work & School sections.

Click the toggle next to the question that you would like to add, and the question will be added to your Custom Intake!

Creating Custom Questions and Document Requests

In order to create Custom Questions and Document Requests, you will need to follow the steps below:

  1. Navigate to the Custom section of the Custom Intake set-up.

  2. Select whether you want the question to:

    1. Provide a space for a response with a short answer.

    2. Provide a space for a response with a long answer.

    3. Be a Document Request.

  3. Type out the Custom Question or Document Request.

  4. Click the Save button.

Text Field (short answer):

With the Text Field (short answer), you can ask a question that would elicit a fairly short response. The text field only accommodates one-line responses (or approximately 75 characters).

Text Area (long answer):

With the Text Area (long answer), you can ask a question that would elicit a longer-form response. The text field accommodates multiple lines of text and can be expanded as necessary.

Document Request:

With Document Requests, clients (or other invitees) may upload multiple files for each Document Request question. Files uploaded will be saved in Docketwise under the Contact and Matter (if selected) to which the Custom Intake is saved.

Adding Custom Questions and Document Requests

After creating a Custom Question or Document Request, click the toggle next to the question that you would like to add, and the question will be added to your Custom Intake within the custom section.

Adding and Using Custom Tabs

Adding Custom Tabs

In order to create Custom Tabs for your Custom Questions and Document Requests, you will need to follow the steps below:

  1. Navigate to the Custom section of the Custom Intake set-up

  2. Select the checkbox for Add custom tabs

  3. Name your new Custom Tab

  4. Click the Add Custom Tab button

Using Custom Tabs

In order to use Custom Tabs, you will first need to add a Custom Question or Document Request to your Custom Intake Template. After adding the Custom Question or Document Request, you simply drag the question to the desired tab.

Sharing/Sending a Custom Intake

In order to share a Custom Intake with a contact, you will first need to create the Custom Intake as a Smart Form by following the steps below:

  1. Click the + Create New ▼ button from your Docketwise dashboard.

  2. Select Smart forms.

  3. Select your Custom Intake Template under the option for Forms that you would like to prepare.

  4. Indicate the client associated with forms.

  5. Indicate the matter associated with the forms (optional).

  6. Click the Create Form button.

After creating the Custom Intake as a Smart Form, it can now be shared with the client via the following methods:

  • Email/SMS Text Invitation

  • Portal Invitation

Importing a Pre-made Custom Intake

We have pre-made custom intakes that you can use to get started with the feature. We have from Basic Intakes to AoS document requests.

  • From the Custom Intake page click on Import Intake.

  • In the window that opens choose the intake that you want by clicking on Import.

  • Once imported you can edit the Intake using the pencil icon and share the intake through a Smart Form.
