## entry: free-trial.trial-signup
- name: Free Trial
- named-by-us: no
- description: New firms self-serve onto a free trial: complete the
  sign-up page and Create Account, confirm via an email from
  no-reply@docketwise.com, create a password on the Account Setup
  page, then log in and provide payment details to start the trial;
  no billing occurs during the trial period (fx-0232, fx-0233,
  fx-0001, fx-0108).
- criterion: Prospective user completes sign-up and account setup ->
  the account starts on a free trial and is not billed during the
  trial period
- sources: fx-0232, fx-0233, fx-0001, fx-0108
- tier: confirmed
- detail: Payment details are required before the trial starts
  (fx-0233). 2FA becomes mandatory once the trial period ends --
  owned by firm-settings.two-factor-authentication.
