# Talking points — tonight, 20 min

Objectives, ranked: (1) burning problem or nice-to-have; (2) how work
actually flows through the firm. Opening and closing are yours — this is
the middle.

Ground rules: don't show or describe atlas. Past tense and specifics.
Signal = numbers, incidents, access granted. Compliments = noise.
Vibe stays beer; the asks are the screen. Score after, cold — not during.

## Part 1 — the money and the labor (~5 min)

- "Seventeen-ish on Docketwise — real number? All-in for the whole stack?"
- "Your complaint was labor, not the subscription. Ballpark: how many
  hours a week on case-management admin, and whose hours?"
  (Do the loaded-cost math live. 10 hrs/wk of mixed staff time is
  $50-150K/yr — that dwarfs the license. Lights him up, or he's never
  counted.)
- "You've looked — Clio, INSZoom, Practice Panther. Did any come close?
  What killed each?"
- "Why now — what brought it to a head?"

## Part 2 — one case, start to finish (~10 min)

- "Pick your bread-and-butter case type. Walk me through the LAST one
  that went out the door — first contact to filing. Who touched it, in
  what order?"
- Interjections when he generalizes: "Who did that step?" / "What system
  was that in?" / "Roughly how long does that take?"
  (Time question on the painful-sounding steps only — three is an audit,
  six is an interrogation.)
- "Where do mistakes actually show up? Who catches them, how late?"
- "Worst one this past year — what made it blow up?"

Listen-fors (ears, not mouth):

| Bet                              | Listening for                             |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Where the labor concentrates     | Admin ring (intake, billing, deadlines,   |
|                                  | chasing docs) vs form prep / QA / rework  |
| Case = one form                  | Forms, or matters bundling many forms     |
| One attorney owns a case         | Handoffs, coverage, who owns a matter     |
| Petitioner + beneficiary only    | Derivatives, joint sponsors, interpreters |
| Linear status lifecycle          | RFEs, amendments, appeals — where rework  |
|                                  | enters                                    |
| Form-vs-source diff is a task    | How errors get caught today, by whom      |
| People recur across matters      | Repeat clients, households, referrals     |

Labor in admin ring = his must-haves list confirmed, form engine is
solving a solved problem. Labor in form prep despite Docketwise = his
list underweights his real pain. Either way: a direction verdict.

## Part 3 — commitment probes (~5 min, pick what fits)

Core four:
- "Can I get an hour next week with whoever actually preps the cases —
  your best paralegal?"  [primary instrument: name + day = green;
  "sometime, sure" = warm deflection]
- "Pull the Docketwise invoice when you get a chance — real number,
  all-in."  [does it arrive this week?]
- "Market tools take too much training, you said. Who at the firm owns
  making twelve attorneys and staff actually use a new system?"
- "When you imagine this built — for the firm, or a product?"
  [then stop talking]

Pocket extras (optional):
- "What have you already tried — spreadsheets, workarounds, throwing a
  person at it?"  [past spend predicts future spend]
- "Who else at the firm has to say yes before real money or time goes
  into this?"  [governance: sole call, or partner fight]
- "If it went well — when would you want the firm running on it?"
  [a date = serious; "eventually" = hobby]
- "Could I sit in the office for half a day and just watch the flow?"
  [the bigger access ask, if the paralegal hour lands easily]
- "Say you do nothing — where's this problem in two years?"
  [urgency: growing pain or steady annoyance]

## Scoring — after, not during

GREEN: invoice numbers from memory; vendor rejection stories; deep
unprompted walkthrough; access granted with name and day; talks in staff
hours and error costs; has a date or a product thesis.

YELLOW: engaged walkthrough, fuzzy numbers; "let me check" on access —
fine IF a date lands within the week.

RED: feature talk; no numbers; warm deflection on both asks; compliments
the idea; "we should do this someday."

Decision rule: RED = break off; the friendship does not convert a
nice-to-have into a project. YELLOW = one more probe (did the paralegal
meeting land?), then decide.
